About

Background

I am an AI Researcher and Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at United International University (UIU). I have taught 13 distinct courses across 8 trimesters since joining as an Instructor in 2024, and was promoted to Lecturer in November 2025. Alongside teaching, I work as a Research Assistant at IAR, UIU: Institute for Advanced Research, contributing to funded research on health informatics and non-invasive diabetes management.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at UIU with a CGPA of 3.94/4.00 (Top 1%, Summa Cum Laude), where my final year project explored data augmentation techniques for text and image classification. Since then, my work has spanned attention-based deep learning for disease detection, multi-task frameworks for food image analysis, and efficiency-oriented vision-language models. I am currently applying to graduate programs to pursue this research further, with the goal of contributing to efficient, interpretable AI systems that are practical to deploy in real-world, resource-constrained settings.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

01

Efficient Machine Learning & Deep Learning

  • Efficient deep learning for biomedical imaging and healthcare, focusing on attention-guided architectures, model pruning, compression, and low-cost inference for deployable intelligent systems.
02

Multi-Task & Multimodal Learning

  • Multi-task and multimodal learning for biomedical imaging and healthcare, combining complementary visual and clinical information to support robust diagnosis, segmentation, and quantitative medical analysis.
03

Vision-Language(-Action) Models & LLMs

  • Efficient medical vision-language models and visual question answering, with a long-term interest in extending multimodal reasoning toward vision-language-action models and embodied AI for healthcare.
